## Brand & Style

This design system channels the mid-century industrial militarism, tongue-in-cheek comic book bombast, and frontier supply-depot charm of 1960s combat logistics. It sits squarely between a top-secret munitions catalogue and an overly macho desert mess hall serving oversized cold cuts. The visual language blends:

- **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Americana:** Heavy cardstock, stamped manila file jackets, stamped metal badges, riveted girders, industrial bevels, and tactile mechanical toggles.
- **Graphic Novel & Comic Paneling:** Dynamic angled badges, 45-degree caution hazard hatching, halftone dot pattern screens, bold ink-weight keylines, and assertive asymmetrical drop shadows.
- **Government Surplus & Munitions Supply:** Gritty, authoritative typography with military stencils, classification banners, and high-impact industrial iconography.

The emotional target is hyper-confident, nostalgic, rugged, and irreverent. Every surface feels physical—like a heavily thumbed field manual or a steel rations crate dropped out of a cargo plane onto New Mexico caliche.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the mid-century corporate-industrial war between RED and BLU factions, set against bleached desert sands and weathered munitions stock.

- **Primary (`#B8383B` - RED Munitions):** Deep, warm brick crimson with burnt rust undertones. Used for destructive actions, RED-aligned inventory, primary action buttons, urgency indicators, and bold framing accents. Paired with deeper shade `#7C2528` for bevel bases and drop shadows.
- **Secondary (`#5885A2` - BLU Steel):** Weathered industrial navy slate. Used for secondary toggles, cold-storage supplies, utility containers, engineering diagnostics, and data-dense secondary tabs. Dark variant `#2B3D4F` anchors pressed states.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A823` - Mann Co. Hazard Amber):** Heavy-machinery yellow-orange used exclusively for critical operational badges, hazard slashes, high-calorie callouts, and limited-run supply crate seals.
- **Neutral (`#2B2625` - Inked Charcoal):** Iron-black printing ink. Replaces sterile digital blacks across borders, typographic strokes, hard drop shadows, and comic framing lines.
- **Canvas & Backing (`#EFE7D3` aged cardstock, `#E1D5B8` manila dossier tone, `#D1C2A5` desert field parchment):** Warm, fibrous paper grounds that eliminate stark digital whites in favor of physical mid-century government printouts.

Halftone overlays and 45-degree yellow/black hazard striping must be applied strictly as functional accents—card headers, warning badges, or active loading trackers—rather than whole-screen wallpapers.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritarian, comic-strip industrial hierarchy:

- **Display & Headlines (`Anton`):** Heavy, condensed, and unapologetic. All headlines render in uppercase. Emulate physical woodblock printing and vintage valve advertisements. Headlines frequently employ a sharp 3px horizontal and 3px vertical offset hard shadow tinted in `#2B2625` or deep crimson `#7C2528`.
- **Editorial & Body (`Work Sans`):** Clean, sturdy grotesque sans-serif with strong mid-century proportions. Highly legible on textured cardstock, even at dense paragraph layouts.
- **Labels, Stamps, Serial Identifiers (`Space Mono`):** Fixed-width typewriter output representing teletype readouts, ration weights, classified stamps, and munitions crate serials. Always uppercase, tight, and tracked out.

Text transformed into classified rubber stamps must be contained in high-contrast rectangular border blocks angled slightly (-2° to 3°) with uppercase `Space Mono` or `Anton` text.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a rugged, structural 12-column fluid grid system pinned inside a max-width industrial containment box of 1280px.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) outer canvas margins. Panels use tight, purposeful gap spacing to feel like bolted-together compartments of a field kitchen or armory wall.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margins. Multi-column inventory shelves collapse into 2-wide card blocks.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Angled badges and heavy headers scale down via dedicated mobile sizes to maintain view integrity without horizontal clipping.

Layout modules mimic comic strips and dossier dossiers: rigid black framing lines separate modules, while occasional asymmetrical tags (e.g., "SANDVICH: RESTOCKS IN 30s") breach panel boundaries to add kinetic comic book momentum.

## Elevation & Depth

This system outright rejects soft, diffused ambient drop shadows and sterile frosted glass. Instead, physical dimension is achieved through **machined bevels, stamped cardstock layers, and ink-bleed hard drop shadows**:

1. **Hard Cast-Ink Shadows:** Elevated interactive surfaces project hard, non-blurred offsets:
   - *Resting Level 1:* `3px 3px 0px #2B2625`
   - *Elevated Level 2 (Hover/Active Dossier):* `6px 6px 0px #2B2625`
   - *Overdrive Level 3 (Modals, Crates):* `10px 10px 0px #2B2625`
2. **Beveled Plate Tiers:** Buttons and interactive badges feature a mechanical bevel. The top and left edge catch an opaque 2px inner highlight (`#FFF` at 35% opacity), while the bottom and right edges display a deep shade lip (`#000` at 40% opacity or `#7C2528`).
3. **Layered Dossier Paper:** Recessed backgrounds (mess hall trays, inventory trays) use `#D1C2A5` with a 2px inset stroke (`inset 0 0 0 2px #2B2625`), while primary cards sit proud on `#EFE7D3` with outer 3px solid ink strokes.
4. **Mechanical Rivets:** Corner joints on modal windows and major container blocks feature four simulated 8px brass/iron screwheads anchored with crossed mechanical crosshair lines.

## Shapes

The shape system is ruthlessly squared off (`0`), prioritizing cut angles, industrial plates, and comic panel rectangles.

- **Zero Curvature:** Default corners are 0px. Buttons, cards, modals, and input fields feature strict right angles.
- **Chamfered / Clipped Edges:** Tactical inventory tags, action-oriented primary badges, and ration cards employ a 45-degree corner notch (clip-path chamfer of 8px to 12px) on the top-right or diagonally opposing corners, referencing punched punch-cards and heavy steel girders.
- **Speech Tail & Dialogue Pointers:** Informational banners, tooltip notifications, and quick bark popups utilize rigid, triangular comic speech-bubble tails projecting directly toward their parent trigger.
- **Heavy Stroke Bounds:** All prominent shapes are circumscribed with a uniform 2px or 3px solid `#2B2625` border to preserve comic book delineation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (RED Munitions):** Background `#B8383B`, text `#EFE7D3` in uppercase `Anton`, 3px solid border in `#2B2625`. Chamfered top-right corner (8px). 4px bottom-right hard drop shadow in `#2B2625`. Top inner highlight line (2px white at 25%). On click/press, offset button 3px down and right while collapsing the drop shadow to 1px for a heavy mechanical "thunk."
- **Secondary (BLU Steel):** Background `#5885A2`, text `#EFE7D3`, structured with the same physical bevel and ink shadow as Primary.
- **Utility / Inactive:** Background `#D1C2A5`, text `#2B2625`, 2px solid border, no chamfer.

### Chips & Badges
- Displayed as stamped canvas tags, ration tickets, or stencil plates.
- Fixed-pitch uppercase `Space Mono` typography.
- Bordered with dashed or continuous 2px `#2B2625` lines.
- Specialized status chips (e.g., "BUFFED", "ROTTEN", "CLASSIFIED") are tilted at -2° angles with rubber-stamp bleed textures.

### Lists & Inventory Trays
- Arranged as steel pegboard or military field crates.
- Each list row separated by a 2px `#2B2625` rule. Alternating rows alternate subtly between `#EFE7D3` and `#E6DABF`.
- Row selection triggers a bright yellow/black 45-degree hazard stripe band along the left margin (8px wide).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Squared 20x20px steel toggles with 2px solid `#2B2625` border and `#EFE7D3` fill. Checked state displays a hand-drawn or heavy industrial thick red "X" (`#B8383B`) with comic inkweight.
- **Radio Buttons:** Diamond-shaped rotated squares (45 degrees) instead of circles. Selected state fills with solid `#5885A2` or `#B8383B` and an inner ink diamond.

### Input Fields
- Heavy cardstock ledger slots: background `#E1D5B8`, inset 2px border `#2B2625`. Text in `Space Mono`. Placeholder text in low-contrast faded charcoal (`#706660`).
- Focus state switches background to stark `#EFE7D3`, adds a 3px hard drop shadow in `#2B2625`, and outlines with a 2px caution-amber `#E5A823` border.

### Cards (Rations & Munitions File)
- Structured like stamped manila dossiers. Features a top tab header containing item classification codes.
- Outer border: 3px solid `#2B2625` with a 5px hard black drop shadow.
- Top card ribbon for culinary or combat stats (e.g., "+300 HP OVER 4 SECONDS") highlighted in halftone dot screen patterns against a warm amber or team crimson background.

### Additional Domain Components
- **The "BREAD BOX" Dossier Drawer:** Accordion container that slides open horizontally like a steel ammunition locker door with riveted hinge graphics.
- **Speech Bubble Alerts:** Urgent notification banners styled with hand-inked borders and a sharp directional speech tail, quoting The Heavy or Mann Co. management in bold comic typography.
- **Hazard Tape Progress Bar:** Segmented loading tracker styled as a continuous ribbon of diagonal caution striping (`#E5A823` and `#2B2625`) that animates continuously across the track.
